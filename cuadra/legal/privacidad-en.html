Privacy policy

Version 1 · Effective October 9, 2026

1. In short

Cuadra doesn't collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app doesn't connect to the internet, has no ads, uses no analytics or trackers, and has no user accounts.

2. What the app stores and where

Your business name, products, prices, quantities, cash counts, and settings. They're stored in the app's private storage on your phone, plus an automatic copy for each of the last 7 days in the same place.

The app also creates a random "installation ID" (for example, K7Q2-M9XD). It doesn't contain your name, phone number, IMEI, or any other device identifier. It only lets an activation code work on a single phone.

3. What we receive

From the app, nothing: it doesn't send data to any server, ours or anyone else's.

When you contact us to activate Cuadra (for example, on WhatsApp or by email), we receive what you send us: your number or email, your installation ID, your business name if you include it, and payment details. We use them only to issue and check your license, support you, and keep our books. We don't sell or share them with third parties unless the law requires it. We keep them for as long as our business relationship lasts and as long as the law requires.

4. When you share something

If you use "Send summary" or "Save a backup", the app opens your phone's share menu and you choose where it goes (WhatsApp, email, another app). What you send is subject to the terms of that service and of whoever receives it. Backups aren't encrypted: keep them somewhere safe.

5. Phone permissions

Cuadra doesn't ask for special permissions: no camera, location, contacts, or microphone. To restore a backup, you pick the file yourself.

6. Security

Your data is protected by your phone's security. We recommend using a screen lock. If you share the phone with others, they'll be able to see the app.

7. Your rights

The app data is in your hands: you can view, correct, or delete it at any time (Settings → Delete everything, or by uninstalling the app). For the activation data we do hold, you can ask us for access, correction, or deletion by writing to michelmiranda683@gmail.com, under the personal data protection laws that apply to you.

8. Children

Cuadra is made for businesses and isn't aimed at children.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, the app will let you know and ask you to accept it again.
