Questions

Is it safe?

Yes. It's signed by Silicon Bay, asks for no permissions (no camera, contacts, or location), and doesn't connect to the internet.

Why isn't it on Google Play?

For now we deliver it directly from this page. That's why Android shows a warning the first time.

It says "Cancelling" when installing

It happens on some phones (especially Xiaomi and Redmi) right after you allow the install. Open Files → Downloads and tap Cuadra.apk again: the second time it installs.

Does it need internet?

No. Only to download it. After that it works offline.

What if I lose or break my phone?

Your data lives only on the phone. In Settings → Backup you save a copy to WhatsApp or anywhere you like, and restore it on the new phone.

How do I activate it after the trial?

In Settings → License, tap "Ask for a code on WhatsApp". We send you a code, you paste it, done.

Will it work on my phone?

On any Android 6 or newer. If it asks you to update "Android System WebView", update it and open Cuadra again.
