What Cuadra is

Cuadra keeps your business's daily stock count and cash count. Instead of a notebook and doing sums by hand, you count what's left and the app does the math.

  1. Everything is saved on the phone instantly, without internet and even during power cuts.
  2. You don't enter the day's sales: they come from what you had, what came in, and what's left.
  3. The cash is checked against sales and tells you if it balances, is short, or is over.

Getting started (owner)

What to do once to get Cuadra ready.

  1. On the welcome screen, enter the business name, the currency, and pick a template (café, shop, or bakery). If none fits, start with no products.
  2. Go to the Products tab. Remove what you don't sell: tap the product, then "Remove product".
  3. Tap each product you sell and enter its selling price and its cost (what it costs you). With the cost, Cuadra works out your real profit.
  4. To add a new one, tap "Add product". Pick the unit: each, lb, kg, L, pack…
  5. If you'll have staff, go to Settings → Team, set an owner PIN, and add each employee. The "Staff and PINs" section explains it.

You can change prices whenever you want. A closed day keeps the prices it was sold at.

The day's count

The Today tab is the count sheet. Each product has three numbers: what you had at the start, what came in, and what's left.

  1. What you had at the start fills in by itself: it's what was left when the previous day closed. On the first day, enter it as "What came in".
  2. In the morning (or when stock arrives), tap "What came in" and enter what you received of each product.
  3. If the business uses a stockroom, you'll see "Stockroom sent…" under each product. Count what really arrived and enter it. If everything arrived, tap "I received everything the stockroom sent".
  4. At the end of the day, tap "What is left", count each product, and enter how many are left. Sales appear by themselves.
  5. If something broke, was thrown out, or was used in the business, tap the product name and enter it under "Waste or own use". That way it doesn't count as a sale.
  6. If a product says "More left than you had", check it: it's almost always a counting mistake or an unrecorded delivery.
  7. With many products, use the search box above the list.

When typing a number, the keyboard's "Next" key takes you to the next product.

The cash count

On the Cash tab you count the money and compare it with sales.

  1. Opening float: the money the drawer started with. It isn't a sale. The owner can set a fixed one in Settings.
  2. Cash in the drawer: enter the total, or tap "Count by bills" and enter how many of each bill there are; the app adds them up.
  3. Transfers: what was paid by transfer or card. That money isn't in the drawer.
  4. Cash taken out: money that left the drawer during the day. Pick the type: expense (ice, transport), stock purchase, or owner withdrawal.
  5. At the bottom you'll see the result: "It balances", "Short", or "Over", with the exact amount.

Only expenses reduce profit. A purchase becomes stock and a withdrawal is the owner's money.

Closing the day and sending it

Closing the day saves it as it is, with its prices, and what's left becomes the next day's start.

  1. When you've finished counting and entered the cash, go to Cash and tap "Close the day".
  2. If a product wasn't counted, the app warns you: it's treated as not sold.
  3. Tap "Send summary" to send it on WhatsApp. If the owner entered their number, it goes straight to them.
  4. Tap "Count sheet" (on the Today tab) to see the full table, like the paper one, and send it as an image.
  5. Only the owner can reopen a closed day to fix it.

The stockroom

The stockroom tracks stored goods: what comes in (purchases) and what goes out (what's sent to the sales area, waste). The owner turns it on in Settings → Team → "Use stockroom".

  1. Opening stock: on the first day, tap "In", choose "Opening stock", and enter what's stored of each product.
  2. Purchases: every time you buy, tap "In", choose "Purchase", the product, the quantity, and (the owner) the unit cost. The product's cost updates by itself.
  3. Send to sales area: tap "Send to sales", enter how much of each product you send, and confirm. The salesperson sees it as "Stockroom sent…".
  4. Waste: what spoiled in the stockroom. Tap "Waste", the product, the quantity, and the reason.
  5. Count: now and then, count the stockroom for real. Tap "Count" and enter what's there. If it doesn't match, the app records the difference.
  6. Tap a product to see all its movements. A wrong movement isn't deleted: the owner voids it and it stays visible.

The real control: the stockroom records what it sends and the salesperson records what they receive. If they don't match, the owner gets a "differences with the stockroom" alert.

Staff and PINs

Several people can use Cuadra on a business phone. Each one signs in with their PIN and only sees what the owner allows.

  1. Go to Settings → Team and tap "Set PIN" to create your owner PIN (4 to 6 digits). Don't share it.
  2. Tap "Add employee": enter their name, a PIN for them, and check what they can do: "Daily count and cash", "Stockroom", or both.
  3. From then on, opening Cuadra shows "Who are you?". Each person taps their name and enters their PIN.
  4. A salesperson only sees today's count and cash. They don't see costs, profit, prices, past days, or settings.
  5. To switch people, tap the sign-out button at the top right.
  6. Enter your WhatsApp number in Settings → Team so the summaries reach you directly.
  7. If an employee forgets their PIN, sign in yourself and set a new one in Settings → Team.
  8. If you forget your owner PIN, on "Who are you?" tap your name, then "I forgot my PIN". Ask Silicon Bay for an unlock code on WhatsApp.

Days and totals

The Days tab shows each day worked, with its sales, its profit, and how the cash ended up.

  1. At the top you see sales for the last 7 days and the month, and the month's shortfall if there was one.
  2. Tap a day to open it on the Today tab and see its detail or its count sheet.
  3. If a day was left open, the app reminds you on the Today tab.

Backups

Everything lives only on this phone. If it gets lost or broken, a backup is the only way to save the data.

  1. Go to Settings → Backup and tap "Save a backup". Send it to your WhatsApp or email, or keep it on another drive.
  2. Do it at least once a week.
  3. To get your data on another phone: install Cuadra, go to Settings → "Restore a backup", and pick the file.
  4. The backup includes products, days, stockroom, and team. The license doesn't move: ask for a new code on the new phone.
  5. The app also keeps a copy for each of the last 7 days on the phone by itself.

Trial and license

You can try Cuadra free for 14 days with everything included.

  1. When the trial ends you can view your data and make backups, but you can't record anything new until you activate it.
  2. To activate: Settings → License → "Ask for a code on WhatsApp". You pay, we send you a code, and you paste it on the same screen.
  3. The code only works on that phone. If you change phones, ask for a new one.

Common problems

What people ask most.

  1. I entered a wrong number: tap it and type it again. If the day is closed, the owner reopens it from the Cash tab.
  2. Sales come out negative or it says "More left than you had": a delivery is missing or the count is wrong.
  3. The cash doesn't balance: check the float, transfers, and cash taken out before assuming money is missing.
  4. When installing it says "Cancelling": tap the file again after allowing the permission.
  5. It says "Your phone needs an update": update "Android System WebView" from your app store.
  6. For anything else, message us on WhatsApp from Settings → Help.
